Overview

This lab gives me a place to work with infrastructure directly rather than only in theory or through classroom examples.

I use it to deploy services, manage networking, test remote access, and understand how different system components fit together in a persistent environment.

What It Shows

Key Work

  • Implemented internal DNS, SSH access, and remote connectivity with Tailscale.
